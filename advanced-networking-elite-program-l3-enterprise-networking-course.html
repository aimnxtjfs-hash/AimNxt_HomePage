What Is the AIMNXT Networking Elite Program?

PROGRAM OVERVIEW

The AIMNXT Networking Elite Program is a 10-week advanced enterprise networking course in India designed for working L2 Network Engineers, Network Administrators, and SREs who want to operate at the L3 level - owning architecture, design, and complex troubleshooting decisions, not just ticket resolution.

MODERN ENTERPRISE NETWORKING STACK

Unlike standard CCNP preparation courses, the Networking Elite Program covers the full modern enterprise stack including Application-Aware Networking, Hybrid Cloud Connectivity, Zero Trust (ZTNA) with Zscaler, Data Center Spine-Leaf Architecture, QoS, VoIP, SD-WAN, and advanced observability with ThousandEyes.

Every topic is reinforced through structured hands-on labs using real enterprise-grade equipment including Cisco Catalyst, Cisco Nexus, Arista, Palo Alto NGFW, FortiGate, and Zscaler ZPA tenants.

CERTIFICATION & CAREER OUTCOME

Graduates receive a formal L3 Competency Certification and leave with documented configurations, implementation procedures, and advanced troubleshooting workflows that can be applied immediately in enterprise production environments.

A 10-Day Intensive Bootcamp option is also available for professionals who cannot commit to the full 10-week schedule.

10-Week Curriculum - What You Learn Week by Week

The curriculum is structured as a journey from the Edge (endpoints and applications) to the Core (data center and cloud) to Strategy (design, troubleshooting, and procurement). Each week builds on the previous - you are not learning isolated topics but a connected enterprise architecture.

Weeks 1-4: The Modern Edge - Endpoint, App-Aware Networking, Virtualization, and Hybrid Cloud

Week 1 - The Endpoint and DNS

Lab: "The Local Stack"

Understand DNS resolution, DHCP, MTU/fragmentation, NIC drivers, Windows routing tables, and troubleshoot issues between endpoint and network infrastructure.

Week 2 - App-Aware Networking

Lab: "The SaaS Squeeze"

Analyze Microsoft Teams, Zoom, SAP and Salesforce traffic using Wireshark. Investigate packet loss, jitter, latency and TCP tuning.

Week 3 - Virtualization and Compute

Lab: "The Hypervisor Bridge"

VMware ESXi 8.0, Proxmox, vSwitch architecture, hypervisor networking and virtual-to-physical integration.

Week 4 - Hybrid Cloud Connectivity

Lab: "The Cloud Tunnel"

Build AWS Transit Gateway connectivity, Hub-and-Spoke VPNs, SD-WAN overlays and enterprise cloud integration.

Weeks 5-7: Zero Trust, Telephony, and Data Center Architecture

Week 5 - Zero Trust and ZTNA

Lab: "Zscaler Shift"

Transition from VPN to ZPA, understand Zero Trust architecture, policy enforcement and user experience implications.

Week 6 - Telephony and QoS

Lab: "The Silent Caller"

SIP troubleshooting, QoS, DSCP, one-way audio analysis and cloud telephony design principles.

Week 7 - Data Center Fabric

Lab: "Spine-Leaf Logic"

Cisco ACI, VXLAN, East-West traffic flows and modern data center fabric architectures.

Weeks 8-10: Design, Documentation, Troubleshooting, and Procurement

Week 8 - Diagrams and Traffic Flow

Lab: "The Mapmaker"

Convert physical infrastructure into logical network diagrams, trust zones and traffic flow maps.

Week 9 - Advanced Troubleshooting

Lab: "The Layer 2 Ghost"

Diagnose STP conflicts, flapping ports and develop L3-level troubleshooting methodology.

Week 10 - Strategic Design and Procurement

Lab: "The BoQ Challenge"

Create enterprise designs, evaluate vendors, prepare BoQs and align technical decisions with business goals.

The 10 Master Tech Troubleshooting Labs - Real Enterprise Scenarios

These are not textbook exercises. Each lab simulates a real production issue that enterprise network engineers encounter. They build the diagnostic intuition that separates L2 technicians from L3 architects.

# Lab Name What You Learn to Do
1 "The Local Stack" Debug Windows routing tables, NIC driver conflicts, and endpoint-level DNS issues that affect application access.
2 "The SaaS Squeeze" Use Wireshark to identify packet loss and jitter in Teams/Zoom traffic; map TCP window scaling behavior.
3 "The Hypervisor Bridge" Configure VMware vSwitches, compare Level 1 vs Level 2 tagging, resolve VM connectivity issues.
4 "The Cloud Tunnel" Build a resilient Hub-and-Spoke VPN with AWS Transit Gateway; troubleshoot overlay routing.
5 "Zscaler Shift" Migrate legacy VPN to Zscaler ZPA; explain why local printer discovery breaks by design in ZTNA.
6 "The Silent Caller" Diagnose one-way audio and jitter on a SIP trunk; implement QoS marking to fix return RTP streams.
7 "Spine-Leaf Logic" Map East-West traffic flows in a Cisco ACI/VXLAN environment; compare to 3-tier campus design.
8 "The Mapmaker" Reconstruct logical topology from physical patch-panel; document traffic flows and security zones.
9 "The Layer 2 Ghost" Identify a flapping port and root bridge conflict in real time; mitigate broadcast storm conditions.
10 "The BoQ Challenge" Design a HA network for a new branch on a $50K budget; produce vendor evaluation and Bill of Quantities.
