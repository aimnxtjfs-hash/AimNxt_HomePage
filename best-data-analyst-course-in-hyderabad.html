Comprehensive Course Structure

The Data Analytics course at AimNxt covers all essential tools and techniques that are crucial for a successful career in data analytics. You'll start by mastering Advanced Excel, where you'll learn advanced spreadsheet functionalities to manage and analyze large datasets. This is followed by a deep dive into SQL, where you'll gain hands-on experience working with relational databases, a skill essential for retrieving, manipulating, and managing data effectively.

Next, AimNxt introduces you to Power BI, a powerful visualization and reporting tool that enables you to present data insights through interactive dashboards and reports. In addition, you will learn Python programming and explore key data analysis libraries such as Pandas, NumPy, and Matplotlib, empowering you to manipulate and visualize data efficiently.

Moreover, Statistics plays a vital role in data analysis, and the course covers both descriptive and inferential statistics. With a solid understanding of statistical measures like mean, variance, and percentiles, you'll be able to extract meaningful insights, validate hypotheses, and make data-driven decisions that are vital for business strategies.
