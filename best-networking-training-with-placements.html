Curriculum

6 Modules · 180 Hours · Zero Fluff

Every topic in NIP was chosen because enterprise interviewers actually ask about it.

1

Networking Fundamentals & CCNA

4 weeks · 40 hours
  • OSI & TCP/IP with real packet captures
  • IP addressing, subnetting, VLSM & summarisation
  • Switching (VLANs, STP, EtherChannel) on real Cisco hardware
  • Routing: OSPF, EIGRP, BGP basics
  • NAT/PAT, ACLs, DHCP, DNS in enterprise scenarios
2

Advanced Routing & CCNP Enterprise

4 weeks · 36 hours
  • OSPF multi-area design, redistribution & filtering
  • BGP advanced policies, communities & path selection
  • SD-WAN & intent-based networking fundamentals
  • MPLS, VRF & L3 VPN concepts
  • CCNP ENCOR-aligned labs on real hardware
3

Firewall Engineering — Palo Alto & Fortinet

4 weeks · 38 hours
  • Palo Alto NGFW zones, security policies & threat prevention
  • App-ID, User-ID & Content-ID
  • Fortinet FortiGate policies, VDOMs & HA clustering
  • Check Point R81 policy management
  • IPSec & SSL VPN, site-to-site tunnels
4

Wireless & Aruba Infrastructure

3 weeks · 24 hours
  • 802.11 standards, RF fundamentals & site surveys
  • Aruba Central & controller-based WLAN deployment
  • SSID design, band steering & load balancing
  • WPA3, 802.1X EAP & wireless security
5

Network Monitoring, NOC & Troubleshooting

3 weeks · 24 hours
  • SNMP, NetFlow, Syslog & NMS (SolarWinds, PRTG)
  • NOC operations, ticketing & escalation
  • Wireshark packet analysis for real incidents
  • ITIL fundamentals for enterprise IT ops
6

Placement Prep & Career Launch

2 weeks · 18 hours
  • ATS-optimised resume for networking roles
  • LinkedIn optimisation & job-search strategy
  • 200+ technical interview Q&A bank
  • Mock interviews with industry professionals
  • Guaranteed interview scheduling with hiring partners
