So we did the boring version instead. We opened every Hyderabad provider page on the first results page that publishes any number at all and wrote the numbers down. Which is the best data analyst course in Hyderabad in 2026?

No single data analyst course in Hyderabad is verifiably the best, because not one provider on Google's first page publishes both its teaching hours and its fee. Judge instead on four checkable things: SQL depth, published hours, a reviewed project, and whether the trainer corrects your queries.

That is not a dodge. It is what happens when you try to do the comparison honestly. Two providers publish hours and no fee. Two publish fees and no hours. One publishes a fee next to three different daily schedules, which means the same Rs 35,000 buys anywhere from roughly 100 to 260 hours of teaching depending on which batch you join.

What you can compare is substance. SQL is the gate in every Hyderabad analyst interview we have seen, and the advert counts in the next section explain why. So the right question is not "which institute is best" but "which syllabus takes SQL past the point where a screening test stops, and who in the room will tell me my join is wrong".

The four tests, in one line each

Total teaching hours in writing. SQL taught to window functions, not just SELECT and JOIN. At least three projects that somebody reviews and sends back. And a trainer who will sit with your query in a free demo before you pay anything.

Why does every institute on this search call itself the best?

Because the phrase is unregulated and the proof is unverifiable. On the live Hyderabad results read on 13 October 2026, FITA Academy self-describes as the number one data analytics training institute, Digifine claims to be the only institute in Asia offering job assistance, and Uncodemy's own blog recommends Uncodemy. None of those is checkable.

The review widgets are worth a longer look, because they are the signal most people actually trust. Google's own results page printed a 5.0 rating from 78,329 reviews against FITA Academy and a 5.0 from 1,28,388 against Uncodemy. Open FITA's own data analytics page and it shows 4.9 from 3,453 ratings. Those are site-wide aggregate widgets covering every course a provider sells, not ratings of the data analytics batch you would join, which is why the numbers disagree with each other.

Placement claims follow the same shape. FITA's page advertises 6,812 students placed last year, 3,000-plus hiring partners and a headline promising an IT job in 60 days, with nothing on the page supporting any of the three. We are not accusing anyone of lying. We are pointing out that none of it can be audited by you, in the twenty minutes you will spend choosing, which makes it useless as a decision input.

What do Hyderabad data analyst adverts actually ask for?

SQL, by a wide margin. On naukri.com, read on 13 October 2026 for Hyderabad and Secunderabad, SQL returns 10,406 open listings against 3,926 for Power BI, 3,709 for Advanced Excel and 3,305 for Tableau. SQL appears in roughly two and a half times as many adverts as the dashboard tool.

That ratio should decide how you read a syllabus. Most course pages in Hyderabad are sold on the dashboard, because a dashboard screenshots well and a GROUP BY does not. The hiring market has the opposite emphasis. If a provider gives you four weeks on Power BI visuals and two weeks on SQL, the brochure is optimised for the brochure.

A caveat we will state rather than hide: keyword counts on a job board are fuzzy. "SQL" picks up developer, testing and data engineering roles too, so the absolute number is inflated for all four tools alike. The ratio between them is the usable part, and the ratio has SQL in front by a distance. We have deliberately left Python out of this table, because a Python search in Hyderabad returns mostly software engineering roles and the number would be meaningless here.

How many data analyst jobs in Hyderabad are open to freshers?

Only 150 of the 3,140 data analyst listings in Hyderabad and Secunderabad accept zero years of experience, which is 4.8 per cent. Of those 150, just 33 sit in the Data Science and Analytics department, 30 are in customer service and operations, and 19 are unpaid internships.

Read that breakdown again, because it is the number no course page in this city prints. Filter the same search to three years of experience and it returns 1,276 listings — roughly 8.5 times the fresher figure. The job market for analysts in Hyderabad is large and healthy. The entry point into it is a side door, and a fair number of the things behind that door are not analyst jobs at all.

This is not an argument against training. It is an argument about what training has to produce. If one in twenty listings will even read a CV with no experience on it, then the course has to hand you something that works instead of experience, and the only thing that does that is published project work with your queries visible in it. A certificate does not clear this filter. Three reviewed projects frequently do.

Why does nobody publish teaching hours, and why does it matter?

Because hours are the only number that turns a fee into a comparison, and almost nobody prints both. ExcelR publishes 190-plus hours over six months but no fee. FITA Academy's FAQ says over 60 hours and prints no fee. Brolly Academy prints three fees and no hours anywhere.

Put ExcelR's 190-plus hours next to FITA's 60 and you have a three-fold difference in teaching time hiding behind the same two words, "data analytics course". Add Quality Thought's one-and-a-half-hour batches and the range gets wider still. A three-month course at one hour a day is about 65 hours. A three-month course at six hours a day is nearer 400. Both are advertised as three months.

So do the division yourself, and ask for the inputs in writing. Quality Thought's exclusive batch at Rs 35,000 works out somewhere between roughly Rs 134 and Rs 359 per taught hour depending on which end of its own published range you land on — our arithmetic from their figures, not theirs. That single number tells you more about value than any review widget on this search, and you can only produce it if the provider gives you both halves.

Get these five numbers in writing before you pay

Total taught hours — not months, not "90 days". Hours, and whether recorded content is counted inside that figure.
Hours per week and batch timing — the same fee buys very different teaching at 1.5 hours a day versus 6.
Hours on SQL specifically — the one block the Hyderabad advert counts say is non-negotiable. Ask whether window functions are covered.
How many projects get reviewed — and reviewed by whom, with written feedback, versus simply submitted.
What the certificate is — an institute's certificate of completion is a record of training, not a vendor credential. Both are fine; conflating them is not.

What should a data analyst course in Hyderabad teach?

Five things: Advanced Excel, SQL to window-function depth, one business intelligence tool taken to data modelling, Python for repeatable analysis, and descriptive statistics. Anything that promises machine learning at entry level is selling a data science syllabus. Three reviewed projects matter more than a sixth tool.

The order matters as much as the list. Excel teaches you to think in rows, columns and keys, which everything else depends on. SQL is where the first screening round lives. A BI tool is how your answer reaches somebody who can act on it. Python comes last, so that you automate work you already understand rather than learning syntax in the dark.

Two things are deliberately not on that list. Machine learning is not an entry-level analyst skill, whatever a brochure implies — it belongs to the data science and machine learning track, and chasing it early is the most common way freshers in Hyderabad delay their first job by six months. R is perfectly good and almost nobody here asks for it. If you are choosing one scripting language, choose Python. There is a fuller sequence, with hour estimates per block, in our guide on how to become a data analyst in India.

Should you learn data analytics in a classroom, online or hybrid?

Classroom still wins in Hyderabad for one practical reason: 144 of the 150 fresher data analyst listings are work-from-office, and only one is remote. If you will interview and work in an office anyway, the habit of explaining a dashboard to a room is worth building in a room.

That said, the format argument is usually a proxy for a different question. What you actually want to know is whether anyone will look at your work. A live online batch with a trainer who reviews queries beats a classroom batch where you copy a screen, and a recorded course at Rs 14,999 gives you neither. The Hyderabad market sells all three as equivalent and they are not.

Hybrid is the honest answer for most working professionals. Learn the Excel and Power BI blocks online around your shifts, then come in for the SQL sessions and the project reviews, which are the two parts where being in the room earns its travel time. For anyone living around KPHB, Kukatpally, Miyapur, Bachupally or JNTU, that commute is short enough that there is no good reason to do the review sessions remotely.

Does a Microsoft PL-300 certification help you get hired?

It helps with keyword screening, not with the SQL round. Microsoft owns the Power BI Data Analyst Associate credential, exam PL-300 runs 100 minutes, lists no prerequisites and must be renewed every 12 months. On Naukri's Hyderabad listings, Power BI appears in 3,926 adverts; PL-300 is rarely named.

What PL-300 measures is worth knowing even if you never sit it, because Microsoft's four assessed areas are a free syllabus audit: prepare the data, model the data, visualise and analyse the data, and manage and secure Power BI. If a Hyderabad course spends its Power BI block on charts and never reaches modelling or workspace management, Microsoft's own exam outline tells you what is missing.

The 12-month renewal is the part people miss. A credential you have to renew every year is a running commitment, not a one-off purchase, and nobody selling you exam prep mentions it. Treat it as a CV keyword and a structured reading list, never as a substitute for three published projects, and remember that AimNxt prepares students for vendor exams but does not administer them.
