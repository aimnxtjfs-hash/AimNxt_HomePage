On 19 October 2026 we read the Hyderabad pages ranking for this phrase and asked one question of each syllabus: where does it start, and where does it stop?

The answers were remarkably consistent. They start at prompting. They stop at retrieval-augmented generation, or at a demo app. Almost none of them teach the Python, the API design or the deployment work that sits on either side of the generative bit, which is most of what the job turns out to be once you are in it.

That gap is the whole reason this page exists. Below are the nine phases AimNxt publishes, in order, with the names taken straight from the course document. Where we quote a competitor, a fee or a salary, the source and the date it was read are listed at the bottom so you can check them yourself.

What should a Gen AI course in Hyderabad actually teach you?

A Gen AI course in Hyderabad is worth paying for only if its syllabus starts before generative AI and continues after it: Python and machine learning first, then retrieval-augmented generation, fine-tuning and agentic AI. The AimNxt Gen AI + Agentic AI with Python programme runs nine phases across 24 to 26 weeks.

Prompt engineering is a week of the work, not a career. What employers in Hyderabad are hiring for is someone who can stand up a FastAPI service, chunk and embed a document set, wire it to a vector store, measure whether the answers are grounded, and then keep the thing running without burning the token budget. Every one of those is ordinary software engineering with a model in the middle.

How long does a Gen AI course in Hyderabad take?

Published Hyderabad Gen AI course lengths ran from 12 weeks to six months when read on 19 October 2026, and the AimNxt Gen AI + Agentic AI with Python programme publishes a 24 to 26 week weekly time grid. IIIT Hyderabad runs 12 weeks at roughly 12 hours each week.

The spread is not really about pace. It is about scope. A twelve-week programme that assumes you already write Python and already know what a gradient is can reach agents quickly, which is exactly why IIIT Hyderabad asks for basic coding and basic mathematics up front and prefers a year of work experience. A three-month course sold to anyone with a degree is covering far less ground than its brochure implies.

AimNxt's grid is published week by week: one week of setup, three on Python, three on data structures and system design, three on machine learning, five on generative AI, two on fine-tuning, four on agentic AI, three on MLOps, and two to three on the capstone. If you are weighing Hyderabad against another city first, our comparison of Gen AI training in Hyderabad and Bangalore covers that question on its own.

Phase 4 — Generative AI with Python + Azure AI Foundry

4–5 weeks. Transformers, tokenization and prompting patterns; Azure AI Foundry hubs and Prompt Flow; embeddings and vector stores; RAG end to end with groundedness evaluation; Content Safety.

Phase 5 — Fine-Tuning & Optimization

2 weeks. Fine-tuning small LLMs via Foundry pipelines or LoRA/PEFT; prompt caching, response streaming, batching; latency, throughput and cost observability.

Phase 6 — Agentic AI in Python

4 weeks. Planning, tools, memory and reflection; LangChain, LlamaIndex and AutoGen agents; function calling and structured outputs; long-term memory; tool authorization, sandboxing and human-in-the-loop.

Phase 7 — MLOps/LLMOps & Production on Azure

3 weeks, marked optional. FastAPI to Azure Container Apps or AKS; Bicep or Terraform; Managed Identity and Key Vault; CI/CD; prompt and token telemetry, safety dashboards.

Phase 8 — Capstone

2–3 weeks. One of an Enterprise RAG Assistant, an Agentic Ops Copilot or Domain QA with fine-tuned policies. Deliverables: architecture doc, IaC, CI/CD, runbook, cost model, red-team report.

Can a fresher or a non-IT professional learn generative AI?

Yes, but only on a course that teaches Python before it teaches prompting: generative AI work is software engineering, and the AimNxt programme spends its first three phases, roughly nine weeks, on Python, data structures and machine learning foundations. IIIT Hyderabad also requires basic coding and basic mathematics.

Be careful with the pages that say a degree in any stream is enough and that familiarity with Python is merely helpful, then teach LoRA adapters in week six. Both statements can be true on the same page and still leave you lost. The honest version is that you do not need Python on day one, but you do need to be willing to write it badly for a month before it clicks.

If your interest is closer to modelling and analysis than to shipping services, the Data Science course in Hyderabad is the better fit and covers deep learning, NLP and LLMs from a different direction; our explainer on data science versus machine learning sets out that split. Nothing stops you doing both, but doing the wrong one first wastes a few months.

What is the difference between generative AI and agentic AI?

Generative AI produces content from a prompt; agentic AI plans a task, calls tools, keeps memory and acts across several steps before returning. The AimNxt syllabus separates them: Phase 4 covers generative AI and retrieval-augmented generation over four to five weeks, and Phase 6 covers Agentic AI in Python over four weeks.

The practical difference is what can go wrong. A generative app returns a bad paragraph and you regenerate it. An agent with tool access can query a database, open a ticket and email somebody before you notice it misread the instruction. That is why Phase 6 spends time on tool authorization, rate limits, sandboxing and human-in-the-loop review rather than only on frameworks.

Hyderabad job adverts have started asking for both in one line, which is partly why courses here advertise both words. Worth checking whether a syllabus actually has a separate agentic block or just a final module called Agentic AI Introduction.

Which tools and cloud platform will you actually use?

The AimNxt Gen AI course tech stack is Python 3.11-plus with FastAPI and pydantic, the openai and Azure SDKs, LangChain, LlamaIndex, FAISS, MLflow, Docker, and Azure AI Search for vector storage. Microsoft has renamed Azure AI Foundry to Microsoft Foundry, so expect both names in current documentation.

Being Azure-native is a real choice with a trade-off, and it is worth understanding before you enrol. It matches how a large share of Hyderabad's enterprise and GCC employers already build, and it means Managed Identity, Key Vault and Container Apps are taught as part of the work rather than left as homework. It also means you learn one cloud deeply instead of three shallowly.

The naming churn is its own small lesson. Microsoft's own documentation records the path from Azure AI Studio to Azure AI Foundry to Microsoft Foundry, with Azure AI Services now called Foundry Tools. Anything in this field that is written down goes stale in months, which is an argument for learning the underlying engineering rather than a tool tour.

What certification does a Gen AI course prepare you for?

The AimNxt Gen AI course content lists AI-900 under Certifications, and Microsoft has since renumbered that exam: the Azure AI Fundamentals certification page was updated on 15 April 2026 and now points at exam AI-901. AimNxt issues its own certificate of completion; Microsoft awards the certification.

Checked on 19 October 2026, the official Microsoft Certified: Azure AI Fundamentals page describes AI-901 as covering AI workloads, machine learning principles on Azure, computer vision, natural language processing and generative AI workloads, for a candidate at the start of an AI career. Not one Hyderabad course page we read mentioned the new code.

Be clear about who awards what. Microsoft, Cisco, EC-Council and CompTIA are the certification bodies for their own credentials. AimNxt is an independent training provider that prepares you for those exams and does not administer anybody else's examination. If a course page implies the institute itself awards a vendor credential, ask which body signs it.

What do generative AI and AI engineering roles pay in India?

AmbitionBox reports an AI Engineer salary range of Rs 3.5 lakh to Rs 27.5 lakh a year in India across zero to six years, from about 1,700 reported salaries, with freshers at Rs 3 lakh to Rs 20 lakh. These are market estimates, not guarantees.

Read the width rather than the midpoint. A range that runs from Rs 3 lakh to Rs 20 lakh at zero years is not describing one job; it is describing a support role and a research-adjacent engineering role that happen to share a title. What moves you up that range is demonstrable work, which is the argument for a capstone you can open and defend rather than a certificate you can only show.
