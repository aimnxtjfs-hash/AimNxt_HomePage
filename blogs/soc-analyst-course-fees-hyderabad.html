Ask what a SOC analyst course costs in Hyderabad and every page you land on answers with its own price. That is not the same as answering the question. What you actually want is the range across the city, what sits behind the gap between the cheapest and the dearest, and whether the number on the page is the number you end up paying.

So we went and read them. On 14 September 2026 we pulled the published fees from every SOC analyst provider ranking for this query in Hyderabad and put them in one table, with dates, so you can check us. We run a SOC programme at KPHB ourselves — treat us as an interested party and verify everything below.

How Much Does a SOC Analyst Course Cost in Hyderabad?

Published SOC analyst course fees in Hyderabad ran from ₹9,999 for recorded self-paced video to ₹45,000 for classroom training when we read institute websites on 14 September 2026. Most live programmes cluster between ₹22,000 and ₹32,000, and several quote that figure before 18 percent GST is added.

That band is wide because the label “SOC analyst course” covers two completely different products. At the bottom you are buying recorded video and a WhatsApp group. At the top you are buying a scheduled seat in a room with a trainer and a login to a SIEM. Comparing the two on price alone tells you nothing useful, which is the whole problem with this search.

Why Does the Same Course Cost ₹9,999 Here and ₹45,000 There?

The gap is almost entirely live instruction and lab access, not syllabus. A ₹9,999 course is video you watch alone; a ₹30,000 classroom course buys scheduled hours with a trainer who answers your question at the moment you are stuck, and your own login to a SIEM. You are paying for contact time, not content.

Syllabus is close to free now. The MITRE ATT&CK matrix, the OWASP Top 10 and Windows event IDs are all documented publicly, and any institute can list them. What nobody can put on a web page is a trainer who has worked SOC shifts telling you why the alert you just closed was actually worth escalating.

Recorded, ~₹10,000

Good as a supplement or a refresher. You get content and no accountability. Completion rates for self-paced video are poor for a reason.

Live online, ~₹25,000

Works well if you get an individual lab login. Works badly if the SIEM only ever appears on the trainer’s shared screen.

Classroom, ₹30,000+

Fixed timings, a physical lab and peers to work alongside. The premium buys attendance pressure as much as it buys teaching.

What Is the Real Total Cost, Including the Certification?

The advertised training fee is rarely the whole bill. Add 18 percent GST wherever the institute quotes tax separately, then the certification exam, which the certification body bills you directly and no institute can include. Microsoft’s SC-200 also expires after 12 months unless you renew it, though the renewal assessment itself is free.

Institutes are not hiding this. It is simply that a training fee and a certification fee are two different transactions with two different companies, and the second one only becomes visible after you have paid the first. Budget for all of it up front:

The training fee, in writing, with the mode and batch named on the same page
18 percent GST, if the quote says “plus taxes” anywhere near the number
The certification exam voucher, paid to CompTIA, EC-Council or Microsoft, not to the institute
A retake, if you need one — a fresh voucher, at full price
Renewal, where the credential expires: SC-200 renews every 12 months
Three months of commute, or a laptop and a connection good enough for live labs

Certification body detail from Microsoft Learn, Security Operations Analyst Associate (SC-200), read 14 September 2026: exam price varies by the country the exam is proctored in, and the certification is valid for 12 months with a free online renewal assessment. Cisco, CompTIA, EC-Council and Microsoft are the certification bodies; AimNxt is an independent training provider that prepares you for their exams and issues its own certificate of completion.

Which certification is worth paying for is a separate decision from which course to buy, and we have gone through the options in detail in our comparison of SOC analyst certifications — CySA+, EC-Council CSA, BTL1 and Cisco CyberOps do not cost the same and do not carry the same weight with Hyderabad employers.

How Long Is a SOC Analyst Course in Hyderabad?

SOC analyst courses in Hyderabad run two to four months, but the number that decides value is instructor-led hours, not calendar months. Indian Cyber Security Solutions publishes 40 hours spread across three months. InfosecTrain publishes 48 hours. Texial advertises two months. AimNxt runs ten modules over roughly three months.

Notice how little those months tell you. Forty hours over three months is two hours twice a week, which suits somebody working full time and is close to useless if you wanted an intensive. The same three-month label can hide 40 hours or 120. Ask for the hour count, and ask separately how many of those hours you spend inside a SIEM rather than looking at slides about one.

How Do You Compare Two Fees That Are Not Comparable?

Divide the total payable, tax included, by the number of instructor-led hours. A ₹25,000 course delivered in 40 hours costs ₹625 an hour; the same ₹25,000 across 90 hours costs ₹278. That single division tells you more than any brochure, and it is the only figure that makes two different institutes comparable.

Two details in that table are worth pausing on. Hyderabad and Bengaluru pay the same band for this role, which removes the usual argument for relocating. And the average has fallen about 2 percent over two years, so nobody should budget for a course on the assumption that entry-level SOC pay is climbing.

The honest caveat is that the course fee is the small number in this equation. The expensive part of a career switch is the three months, and the risk is not that you pay ₹30,000 for a bad course — it is that you spend a quarter of a year on one and come out unable to explain a Windows 4625 log to an interviewer. That is what the hour count and the lab access are actually protecting you from.

Does a Higher Fee Mean Better Placement Support?

No. Fee and placement outcome are not correlated in Hyderabad, because no institute at any price controls an employer’s hiring decision. Read “100 percent placement” as marketing whatever it costs. What an institute can genuinely commit to in writing is interview opportunities, resume support and mock interview practice.

The one fee-related question worth asking is whether placement support is bundled or sold separately, because a handful of programmes price an internship or a placement track as an add-on and it does not appear in the headline number. Ask directly: is the interview support included in this fee, or is it another line item later?

Our own commitment, stated precisely so you can hold us to the wording: the AimNxt Job Interview Guarantee programme guarantees interview opportunities through our hiring-partner network until you are placement-ready. It does not guarantee a job offer, a placement or any salary — that depends on your skills and how you perform in the interview. If another institute’s wording is looser than that, ask them to tighten it before you pay.
