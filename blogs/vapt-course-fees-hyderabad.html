Asking what a VAPT course costs in Hyderabad should be a five-minute job. It is not, and the reason is simple: most institutes will not tell you.

We read the whole of Google’s first page for “vapt course fees in hyderabad” from India on 27 September 2026. Nine providers. Four of them publish no number anywhere on the page. One says, in writing, to ask before visiting. Three publish a figure with a line through the old price next to it. And the depth leader, a page running roughly 45,000 words, was still advertising a “next batch” date of 25 May 2026 — four months stale on the day we read it.

Not one of the nine does the thing that actually matters, which is to put the training fee next to the certification exam fee. That omission is why so many people budget ₹35,000 for “VAPT” and then discover the exam they were aiming at costs three to five times the course. This page fixes that. Every figure below is dated, sourced, and was read on the day of publication.

How Much Does a VAPT Course Cost in Hyderabad in 2026?

Published VAPT and ethical hacking course fees in Hyderabad run from ₹9,999 for recorded video access to ₹45,000 for classroom training, with ₹25,000 to ₹35,000 the common live-training band. Those are the nine providers’ own published figures, read on 27 September 2026. Certification exams are charged separately and cost far more.

Two things make that range less useful than it looks. First, almost nobody states how many live instructor hours the fee buys, so the numbers are not comparable. Second, almost nobody states whether 18% GST is on top. Both are fixable by asking one question each, and we have put those questions in a checklist further down.

The number that gets left out: on 27 September 2026 the CEH exam voucher was listed at US$1,199 on the EC-Council store — about ₹1.15 lakh at ₹95.78 to the dollar. That single voucher costs more than every Hyderabad training fee on this page put together. No Hyderabad fees page we read mentions it.

What Does a VAPT Course Cost Per Instructor Hour?

Only two Hyderabad providers publish both a fee and an hour count, and per instructor hour they differ by roughly 3.5 times: Hacker School works out at about ₹167 an hour for 72-plus hours at ₹12,000, and Nexson IT Academy at about ₹583 an hour for 60-plus hours at ₹35,000. Everyone else publishes one number or neither.

That gap is the whole argument about VAPT fees in one line. It is not a story about one institute being greedy and another being generous; it is a story about nobody being asked to show the arithmetic. Add GST to the Hacker School figure and it becomes roughly ₹197 an hour. Nexson’s page does not say whether its ₹35,000 is inclusive.

What Does the VAPT Course Fee Not Include?

A Hyderabad VAPT course fee normally covers live sessions, lab access during the course and a completion certificate — it almost never covers the certification exam, GST, exam retakes, or lab subscriptions after the batch ends. Only one of the nine providers we read states GST separately, and none prices a retake.

None of that is dishonest in itself. The certification body bills the exam, not the institute, so an institute genuinely cannot bundle it unless it buys vouchers. The problem is silence: a fee page that lists neither the exclusion nor the number leaves you to discover it after paying.

The certification exam

Billed by EC-Council, OffSec, INE or CompTIA directly. Usually the largest single cost in the whole plan.

18% GST

Commercial training attracts GST. ₹35,000 quoted exclusive is ₹41,300 paid. Ask which one you were shown.

Exam retakes

A failed attempt means buying a fresh voucher at full price unless the vendor plan included a second attempt.

Lab access after the batch

Practice platforms and cloud ranges are usually subscriptions. Ask what happens to your access the week the course ends.

How Do You Get a VAPT Certification?

There is no exam anywhere called VAPT — you certify through a named vendor exam instead, most commonly CEH, OSCP, eJPT or PNPT, and a training institute prepares you for one of those. EC-Council and OffSec are the certification bodies; a Hyderabad institute issues only its own certificate of completion.

This matters for your budget because the phrase “VAPT certification” on an institute’s page can mean two completely different things: a course completion certificate printed by the institute, or preparation for a vendor exam you buy separately. The first is included in the fee. The second is not. We worked through the four realistic exam routes and what each one suits in our guide to the best VAPT certifications for 2026.

Is There a Free VAPT Course?

Yes, free VAPT learning exists and it is genuinely good — TryHackMe and Hack The Box free tiers, OWASP documentation, DVWA and Metasploitable, and Nessus Essentials for five IP addresses — but none of it gives you a trainer, a reviewed report, or an interview. Free covers the tools, not the feedback.

Be clear-eyed about what you are paying for when you do pay. You are not buying access to Nmap; that is free and always was. You are buying someone experienced watching how you approach a target, telling you your enumeration was lazy, and marking up the report you wrote. If a paid course does not include a reviewed written report, you are paying for a video you could have found.

What you can genuinely learn at zero cost

Guided practice: TryHackMe and Hack The Box free tiers cover a real beginner path, rate-limited rather than crippled.
Vulnerable targets: DVWA, bWAPP, Metasploitable, OWASP Juice Shop and VulnHub images all run on your own laptop.
Scanning: Nessus Essentials is free but capped at five IP addresses; OpenVAS and OWASP ZAP have no cap.
Reference material: the OWASP Top 10, PTES and NIST SP 800-115 are free and are what interviewers quote.
What stays out of reach: method correction, a reviewed report, mock interviews, and an introduction to a hiring manager.

What Actually Makes One VAPT Course Cost More Than Another?

Four things move the price honestly: live instructor hours, whether the lab is a shared cloud range or a real isolated environment, how much of the trainer’s time goes into reviewing your written findings, and whether a certification exam voucher is bundled. Everything else — EMI offers, struck-through discounts, review widgets — is presentation, not cost.

Report review is the one people underestimate. Marking up a trainee’s 20-page penetration test report properly takes an experienced tester an hour or more per student per iteration, and it is the part of the job a client actually sees. That is why the VAPT & Ethical Hacking programme at AimNxt keeps Report Writing & Professional Skills as a full module rather than a closing lecture, and ends on a capstone engagement you write up and present. If a cheaper course never reads your writing, the saving is real but so is the gap.

Who is in the room

A working tester’s time costs more than a video playback operator’s. Ask who teaches your batch, by name, and what they test for a living.

What the lab really is

A shared browser-based range is cheap to run. An isolated environment where you can break Active Directory and rebuild it is not.

Whether writing is marked

Reviewed reports are the most expensive thing a course can offer and the most valuable in an interview. Ask how many you will submit.

Whether a voucher is inside

A bundled exam voucher can add a lakh or more to a fee legitimately. An unbundled one can make a cheap course expensive later.

What Should You Verify Before You Pay Anything?

Get six things in writing before any payment: total fee inclusive of GST, the number of live instructor hours, who teaches the batch, what happens to lab access after it ends, whether an exam voucher is included, and the refund position if you drop out. A provider that will not put those in writing is answering you already.

Two further checks cost you nothing and caught real problems on this SERP. Look at the advertised batch date: one page was still promoting a 25 May 2026 start when we read it in late September. And look at where the star rating comes from — a widget reading 4.9 from 48,765 reviews is a site-wide figure across every course a company sells in every city, not a verdict on the Hyderabad VAPT batch you are considering.

1. Total, inclusive of GST

One number, on paper or WhatsApp. “Plus taxes” on an 18% base is ₹6,300 on a ₹35,000 fee.

2. Live instructor hours

Not “3 months”. Hours, and how they split between lecture and supervised lab.

3. Who teaches your batch

By name, with what they test professionally. Meet them in the demo, not a counsellor.

4. Lab access after the course

Ask the end date, in writing. Most cloud ranges switch off with the batch.

5. Exam voucher: in or out?

If bundled, ask which exam and whether a retake is covered. If not, budget it yourself.

6. Refund position

What you get back if you leave in week two. Silence here is the answer you should act on.
