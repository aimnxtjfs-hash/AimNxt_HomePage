Company Profile:

Nipun Net Solutions Pvt. Ltd. is a leading IT Infrastructure and Network Solutions provider, established in 2002 and headquartered in Hyderabad, India. With a strong presence across major Indian cities and over two decades of industry experience, Nipun delivers cutting-edge solutions in Infrastructure as a Service (IaaS), networking, cybersecurity, wireless technologies, and smart digital services. The company is recognized for its expertise in executing large-scale projects, smart city deployments, and offering managed services to a wide range of enterprise clients. Nipun is an ISO 27001:2013 certified organization and a trusted partner to global technology leaders including Cisco, Fortinet, Aruba, and more.

Job Summary:

We are seeking a highly skilled and experienced L3 Network Engineer to join our growing technical team at Nipun Net Solutions Pvt. Ltd. The ideal candidate will be responsible for advanced network troubleshooting, design, implementation, and maintenance of LAN/WAN/WLAN environments, with deep expertise across routing, switching, firewalls, wireless, and network automation.

Key Responsibilities:

  • Implement and maintain LAN / WAN and WLAN equipment’s from various vendors
  • Upgrade network hardware and software components as required
  • Switch configuring and troubleshooting VLANs, STP, RSTP, MST
  • Router configuration with static and dynamic routing protocols RIP / OSPF / EIGRP
  • Firewall Configuration GRE / IPsec / NAT / PAT
  • Excellent knowledge of Cisco Wireless Controllers and Aruba ClearPass

Preferred Qualifications:

  • Minimum 5 years of experience in enterprise network environments
  • Good communication and team management skills
  • Strong knowledge in routing protocols and firewall technologies
