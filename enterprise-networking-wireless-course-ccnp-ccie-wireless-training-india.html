What is the AIMNXT Enterprise Networking Wireless Training Program?

Purpose

The AIMNXT Enterprise Wireless Mastery is an advanced wireless engineering program built for network engineers with foundation wireless experience who want to design, deploy, assure, and evolve enterprise wireless infrastructure at a professional level.

Curriculum - 10 core modules

The program covers the complete enterprise wireless lifecycle across 10 core modules - from initial RF planning and capacity modelling with Ekahau, through C9800 WLC implementation and policy configuration, 802.1x authentication with Cisco ISE, Software Defined Access (SDA) wireless integration, Catalyst Centre assurance dashboards, Cisco Spaces for IoT and location services, and enterprise wireless evolution into Wi-Fi 6E and Wi-Fi 7.

What sets it apart

What distinguishes this program from all other enterprise wireless training in India is direct access to real hardware: a live Cisco Catalyst 9800 Wireless LAN Controller, the Ekahau RF survey tool (the industry standard for enterprise Wi-Fi planning), and full Catalyst Centre plus Cisco ISE integration - not packet tracer or dCloud simulations.

Exam alignment

The curriculum maps directly to CCNP Wireless and CCIE Wireless exam blueprints, with lab scenarios designed around real exam objectives. Graduates leave with documented configurations and RF survey workflows applicable immediately in enterprise environments.

10 Core Modules - Full Lifecycle Enterprise Wireless Coverage

The Enterprise Wireless Mastery curriculum maps every module to a real enterprise deployment phase. You are not learning isolated topics - you are learning a connected, deployable wireless engineering workflow from initial RF design through live assurance and security enforcement.

Module 06 - Wireless Network Design

Master real-world enterprise scenarios for capacity modelling and RF architecture. Understand how building materials, antenna placement, AP density, and interference sources affect coverage and performance. Design the wireless network before deployment begins.

Module 09 - RF Surveys with Ekahau

Conduct predictive planning sessions using Ekahau - the industry-standard RF survey tool used by Cisco-certified wireless architects worldwide. Perform post-deployment validation using heatmaps. Learn how to document RF survey findings in the professional format enterprise clients require.

Module 01 - C9800 WLC Implementation and Troubleshooting

Get direct hands-on access to the Cisco Catalyst 9800 Wireless LAN Controller - the current enterprise standard, replacing AireOS. Covers implementation, policy configuration, fault injection, and deep-dive controller operations, including Catalyst Centre (DNAC) integration.

Module 02 - Quality of Service (QoS)

Design, marking, and policy enforcement across enterprise wireless infrastructure. Understand DSCP classification for voice, video, and data traffic - ensuring consistent performance for real-time applications.

Module 03 - 802.1x Authentication with Cisco ISE

Implement end-to-end 802.1x with Cisco ISE. Covers client profiling, posture assessment, dynamic VLAN assignment, and zero-trust wireless access control. This module directly addresses the most common security gap in enterprise wireless deployments.

Module 04 - Branch and Guest Wireless Solutions

Configure FlexConnect for branch deployments, anchor WLC configurations for guest wireless, and captive portal setup - the three most common branch wireless scenarios in enterprise environments.

Module 05 - Software Defined Access (SDA) Wireless

Integrate enterprise wireless into a Cisco SDA fabric environment. Covers wireless fabric integration, policy enforcement, VXLAN-based segmentation, and automation-ready architecture. SDA wireless is increasingly standard in new enterprise campus deployments.

Module 07 - Catalyst Centre Assurance

Move from reactive troubleshooting to proactive network management. Understand how to use Catalyst Centre dashboards, AI-driven health scores, and assurance data to identify RF interference, roaming failures, client health issues, and security anomalies before users report them.

Module 08 - Cisco Spaces

Location services, IoT device integration, and intelligent building connectivity using Cisco Spaces. Covers indoor wayfinding, asset tracking, and real-time occupancy analytics - increasingly requested by enterprise clients in smart building projects.

Module 10 - Wireless Technology Evolution

Understand Wi-Fi 6E, Wi-Fi 7, and emerging enterprise wireless standards. What changes in the 6 GHz band, how Wi-Fi 7 multi-link operation (MLO) reduces latency, and how enterprise wireless architects must adapt their RF design and capacity planning approaches for next-generation deployments.
